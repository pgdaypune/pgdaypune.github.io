    title: "Keynote: Postgres 30 Years In: How We Got Here and Where We Are Going"
    speaker: "Tom Kincaid"
    abstract: >-
      In this talk, the speaker looked back at how Postgres grew from a small
      project into a top choice for building apps today. He then looked ahead at
      what Postgres 19 brings, why it is becoming popular for AI work, and what
      challenges lie ahead.

  - start: "10:10"
    end: "10:50"
    title: "Postgres as the System of Record for AI Agents: Memory, State, and Tool Outputs"
    speaker: "Abhishek Oberoi, Nadeem Khan"
    abstract: >-
      In this talk, the speakers explained why PostgreSQL, not a mix of Redis, JSON
      files, and separate vector stores, should hold an AI agent's memory and state.
      They showed how JSONB, pgvector, row level security, and transactions can work
      together in one schema, and discussed where a database still is not the right fit.

    end: "12:10"
    title: "Know Your PL/pgSQL: What Happens Between BEGIN and END"
    speaker: "Deepak Mahato, Sneha Gohil"
    abstract: >-
      In this talk, the speakers explained how PL/pgSQL actually runs behind the
      scenes, which helps explain why code that seems fine can turn slow or buggy
      later. They covered loops, settings that affect performance, caching issues,
      common mistakes with exceptions, and other traps, shown through real code
      examples.

  - start: "12:25"
    end: "13:05"
    title: "Logical Failover Slots: Keeping Replication Alive Through a Failover"
    speaker: "Ashutosh Sharma"
    abstract: >-
      In this talk, the speaker covered a Postgres 17 feature that keeps logical
      replication slots in sync on standby servers, so subscribers can keep going after
      a failover without needing a full resync. He also showed the setup steps and
      how to check that it works.

  - start: "14:30"
    end: "15:10"
    title: "From Insight 1.0 to 2.0: Solution Engineering PostgreSQL in a Regulated, Nation Scale Tax Platform"
    speaker: "Hari Kiran"
    abstract: >-
      In this talk, the speaker shared a first hand account of moving a national
      tax system to PostgreSQL. He covered checking against government rules,
      studying the old system, planning the new setup for reliability, and balancing
      performance and security with AI features still being worked on.

  - start: "15:15"
    end: "15:25"
    type: break
    title: "CortexRidge & Percona Sponsor Talk (5 min each)"

  - start: "15:25"
    end: "16:05"
    title: "A Benchmarking Case Study of PostgreSQL Locks, Partitions, and Peak Load"
    speaker: "Vishal Sawale"
    abstract: >-
      In this talk, the speaker showed how a certain type of lock can silently limit
      performance under heavy write traffic, based on real benchmark tests. He
      explained how to spot this problem and how table partitioning can fix it, along with
      tips on choosing the right amount of partitioning.

  - start: "16:05"
    end: "16:30"
    type: break
    title: "Hi Tea Break"

  - start: "16:30"
    end: "17:10"
    title: "Bi Directional CDC for Oracle to PostgreSQL Migration"
    speaker: "Debendra Saha, Anuj Sharma"
    abstract: >-
      In this talk, the speakers described how a large satellite and telecommunications company moved from
      Oracle to PostgreSQL across 16 countries while keeping Oracle as a safe backup using two-way data replication.
      They covered how conflicts were avoided, how failures were handled, and how both databases were kept in sync.

    end: "17:20"

    title: "NewT & Yugabyte Sponsor Talk (5 min each)"

  - start: "17:20"
    end: "18:00"
    title: "Surviving ALTER EXTENSION UPDATE"
    speaker: "Mukund Maheshwari, Pushkar Khadilkar"
    abstract: >-
      In this talk, the speakers looked at what really happens when a Postgres
      extension is upgraded, and why upgrade scripts often fail in production. They
      covered common mistakes, how Postgres picks an upgrade path internally, and
      shared a checklist for building extensions that upgrade safely.
